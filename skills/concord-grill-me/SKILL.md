# Clarify decisions

Use this method to expose and settle material decisions, not to add a workflow
step or gate. It grants no scope, approval, or authority to change state.

This method adapts grilling by Matt Pocock; the upstream MIT notice is in this
skill's `LICENSE`.

[Portable method admission](https://github.com/Sharper-Flow/concord/tree/main/.concord/law/decisions/CD-0246-concord-ships-portable-engineering-methods.md)
defines how Concord ships this guidance.

## Work through the decisions

1. Establish the intended outcome and the decisions that could materially change
   it. Reuse settled decisions and their evidence; do not reopen them for
   reassurance.
2. Map the unresolved decisions and their dependencies. Identify which decisions
   are ready because their prerequisites are settled. An answer can remove or
   reveal a dependent decision.
3. Resolve facts from available evidence and investigation first. Decide within
   your authority and assigned scope; state the reason and consequences in the
   existing work record. Do not ask the operator for a fact you can establish.
4. When a material decision needs the operator, follow the shared `asking.md`
   instructions for authority, approval, the question route and its pacing.
   Give your recommendation and the consequences. A question that depends on an
   open decision waits for that decision.
5. Revisit the dependency map after each resolution. Continue only while a
   material decision remains unresolved. Stop when those decisions are settled;
   neither exhaustive interviewing nor blanket confirmation is required.

Follow the shared `records.md` instructions for recording decisions and
approval evidence. A question or answer does not itself grant implementation
authority. If evidence or an admitted decision route is missing, report the
gap rather than invent an answer.
