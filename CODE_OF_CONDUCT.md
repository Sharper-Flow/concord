# Code of Conduct

Concord contributors must keep project spaces welcoming, technically focused,
and safe for people with different backgrounds and experience.

Expected behavior:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks;
- welcome useful corrections and acknowledge mistakes;
- protect private information and avoid harassment, discrimination, and
  unwanted contact.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainers
through GitHub. Maintainers may clarify, moderate, or remove participation
when needed to protect the project community.
