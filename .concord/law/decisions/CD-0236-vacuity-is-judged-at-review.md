# CD-0236: Vacuity is judged at review

## Decision

### D1. Review judges vacuity

Independent Review judges whether the work delivered its stated outcome,
including whether the claimed result already held before execution. A
caller-supplied label is not proof of the state of the world. Concord does not
decide vacuity through an approval-time predicate check.
